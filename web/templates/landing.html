Standardized stimulus

A content-addressed target set, shown 1:1 at native pixels on an Apple display (an iPhone screen is fine). Apple mass-produces tightly calibrated panels, so everyone ends up photographing the same picture. Pixel-exact or invalid — no scaling, ever. Every frame is a controlled probe: ramps, spectra, skin tones, slanted edges, point sources.

Real extraction

Fiducial registration, per-channel tone curves, slanted-edge MTF, radial vignette, veiling-glare and PSF crops. RAW and camera-JPEG are separate measurement classes; RAW earns higher confidence.

Network effect

Every capture makes the library denser. The moat is measurements plus contributors, not image hoarding — but originals are archived so each methodology improvement re-processes the whole set.
